6. CABIN TIDY QUALITY FILTER
======================================================================

The current sample selected a vehicle-specific replacement part:
"Center Console Lid with Cup Holders Solid Black Fits 1999-2007 Silverado Sierra"

For Cabin Tidy, broad organization/problem-solving items should rank above:
- vehicle-specific replacement parts
- fitment-specific trim/body parts
- OEM replacement components
- model-year-specific repair parts

Do NOT delete/exclude these products from the store catalog.
Only lower their homepage featured-product rank.

Add a generic homepage merchandising suitability score, for example:

- organizer
- storage
- trash
- holder
- cargo
- seat back
- trunk
- console organizer
- cup holder organizer
- visor organizer
- travel organizer
negative/rank-down signals:
- "fits 19xx-20xx"
- make/model names tied to fitment
- replacement
- trim
- lid
- panel
- OEM / OE
- direct-fit
- part number style titles

This scoring should be configurable/profile-aware and not Cabin-Tidy-only core hacks.

BALANCED_CATEGORIES selection order should consider:
1. eligibility
2. merchandising suitability
3. category diversity
4. freshness

Do not allow a low-suitability replacement part to displace a high-suitability organizer if a suitable organizer is available.
